Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how William D. Clifford, Esq., operating as Clifford Construction Mediation, uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on cliffordconstructionmediation.com. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function efficiently and provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining until deleted) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

3. Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Site to function and cannot be switched off. They include cookies set by Termly to remember your cookie preferences and consent choices.

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to collect information about how visitors use the Site, including pages visited, time spent, and referring sources. These cookies are set only after you have consented to analytics cookies.

Functional Cookies

Calendly is our appointment scheduling provider. If you consent to performance cookies, our scheduling tool loads directly on this Site and Calendly may set cookies on your device to operate the booking interface. If you do not consent, the scheduling tool is not loaded and no Calendly cookies are set — selecting "Request a date" will instead open Calendly's own website in a new tab.

4. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Consent Preferences" in the footer of our main site. You can also control cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect the functionality of the Site.

5. Global Privacy Control

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If your browser sends a GPC opt-out signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request under applicable law.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — analytics and traffic measurement (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Tag Manager — tag management
  • Calendly — appointment scheduling (Calendly Privacy Notice). Calendly's scheduling widget is loaded only after you accept performance cookies.
  • Termly — consent management
