2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Request a mediation date through our third-party scheduling page (Calendly), including the party, counsel, conflicts-check, and logistical information requested on that form
  • Send us an email or contact us by telephone

This may include your name, email address, phone number, company or firm name, the identities of parties and counsel involved in a matter, and information you choose to provide about the nature of a dispute.

The Site does not host a contact or intake form. All written inquiries reach us by email.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect IP address and general geographic location, browser type and device information, pages visited and time spent on the Site, referring website or search engine, and log and usage data. This information is collected only where you have consented to analytics cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule mediation sessions
  • To conduct conflict-of-interest checks before accepting an engagement
  • To prepare the Agreement to Mediate and related documents
  • To communicate with you regarding your matter
  • To improve the functionality and content of the Site
  • To comply with applicable legal and professional obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. We use Termly to manage cookie consent. You may withdraw or modify your cookie consent at any time by clicking "Consent Preferences" in the footer of our main site. For full details, please see our Cookie Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Calendly — third-party appointment scheduling. When you request a mediation date you are directed to a page hosted by Calendly, where the information you provide is collected and stored by Calendly and transmitted to us. Calendly's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy notice.
  • Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) — website traffic analysis, loaded only after you consent to analytics cookies
  • Termly — cookie consent management
  • Google Workspace (Gmail and Calendar) — our email and internal calendar. Confirmed sessions are recorded on our internal calendar and correspondence is handled through our Workspace email.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion or correction, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, email us at info@cliffordconstructionmediation.com with the subject line "Privacy Request," or write to us at the address in Section 13. Please describe the right you wish to exercise and provide enough information for us to verify your identity. We will respond within 45 days.

7. GDPR Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data.

To exercise these rights, email us at info@cliffordconstructionmediation.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We may ask for information sufficient to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Global Privacy Control

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If your browser sends a GPC opt-out signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request under applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for a minimum of three years, consistent with professional responsibility obligations applicable to attorneys practicing in Pennsylvania.

Booking request information collected through our scheduling page is retained for the duration of the matter and for a minimum of three years thereafter. Requests that do not result in a scheduled mediation are deleted within twelve months.

10. Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Please do not send confidential or privileged information by email or through our scheduling page. Information transmitted to us before an Agreement to Mediate has been executed is not protected by mediation confidentiality under 42 Pa. C.S.A. §5949.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

12. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Your continued use of the Site constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
