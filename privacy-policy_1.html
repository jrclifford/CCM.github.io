2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you schedule a mediation session, complete the mediation intake form, or contact us directly. This may include your name, email address, phone number, company or firm name, and a description of your dispute.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, we may automatically collect IP address and general geographic location, browser type and device information, pages visited and time spent on the Site, referring website or search engine, and log and usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule mediation sessions
  • To prepare the Agreement to Mediate and related documents
  • To communicate with you regarding your matter
  • To improve the functionality and content of the Site
  • To comply with applicable legal and professional obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. We use Termly to manage cookie consent. You may withdraw or modify your cookie consent at any time by clicking "Consent Preferences" in the footer of our main site. For full details, please see our Cookie Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Calendar — appointment scheduling
  • Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) — anonymous traffic analysis
  • Termly — cookie consent management
  • Google Workspace — email and calendar services

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion or correction, opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights. Contact us at info@cliffordconstructionmediation.com to exercise these rights. We will respond within 45 days.

7. GDPR Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data. Contact us at info@cliffordconstructionmediation.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Global Privacy Control

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If your browser sends a GPC opt-out signal, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request under applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for a minimum of three years, consistent with professional responsibility obligations applicable to attorneys practicing in Pennsylvania.

10. Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
