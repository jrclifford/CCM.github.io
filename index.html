About

A mediator with unmatched construction law depth

William D. Clifford, Esq. has been a Shareholder and Director at Dickie, McCamey & Chilcote, P.C. since 1984, where his practice centers on the drafting, negotiation, litigation, and mediation of construction and surety claims. His construction practice has involved airports, stadiums, convention centers, schools, bridges, highways, public housing, steel mills, electrical power plants, commercial office buildings, and prisons.

"The secret to resolution is persistence and creativity — and never giving up without using every available tool."

Process

How a mediation works

The process is built around preparation. By the time the parties are in the room, every position has been reviewed and the day is spent working toward settlement.

1

Pre-Mediation Statements

Parties submit and exchange written statements at least one week before the session. Bill reviews all materials in advance.

2

Ex Parte Conferences

Bill conducts confidential telephone conferences with counsel for each party to understand positions and objectives.

3

Mediation Session

A brief joint plenary session is followed by breakout sessions. Parties are guided through the facts and then toward monetary positions and resolution.

4

Persistent Resolution

Bill stays with a mediation until every tool has been used — brackets, mediator proposals, and candid evaluations of each side's position.

Services

Construction dispute resolution services

From ground-breaking to post-completion litigation, Bill has mediated disputes at every phase of construction and among every kind of party.

75 of Bill's 150+ mediated cases involve owner-contractor disputes — covering scope, change orders, payment, and project delivery on projects of every scale including airports, stadiums, power plants, and large residences.

50 cases spanning scope-of-work disputes, payment and retainage, scheduling conflicts, and back-charge claims between general contractors and their subcontractors or suppliers.

Experienced in standard of care disputes, errors and omissions claims, and design-build issues between owners and architects or engineers.

Deep expertise in liquidated damages, schedule impacts, acceleration claims, and inefficiency disputes arising from project delays and disruptions.

Skilled mediation of surety bond claims, insurance coverage disputes, subrogation claims, and surety takeover issues involving sureties, owners, and contractors.

Resolution of unpaid contract balances, mechanic's lien disputes, False Claims Act issues, and ERISA claims — preserving cash flow and business relationships.

Video mediation for parties who cannot meet in person, held with the same structure as an in-person session — pre-mediation conferences, a joint opening, and private caucuses.
